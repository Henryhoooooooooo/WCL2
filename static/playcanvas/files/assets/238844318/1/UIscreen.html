Joysticks

You have two types of joysticks, with default option you can use left joystick to move in forward, backward, left, and right directions and rotation with right joystick or with second type you can use single joystick move in forward, backward, left, and right directions and rotation with mouse or touch. You can also hide joystick and use WASD and arrow keys to move around.

Vertical Movement

Toggle this button to lock vertical camera movement in the 3D scene to get a fixed perspective or focusing on horizontal exploration without distractions.

Click To Move / Teleport

Click anywhere on the floor to smoothly move your character in the 3D scene. Ideal for quick navigation.

Light Intensity

You can change the light intensity

FOV Control

FOV (Field of View) adjusts how much of the scene you want to see on screen, Higher values show more surroundings and Lower values offer a focused view, Find your comfort zone.

Movement Power

This slider controls how quickly the camera moves in forward, backward, left, and right in response to mouse, joysticks, WASD or arrow keys. Increase for faster reactions, decrease for more precise control.

Mouse Sensitivity

This slider controls how quickly the camera rotates in response to mouse or joysticks movement. Increase for faster reactions, decrease for more precise control.
